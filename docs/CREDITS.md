# Credits

This project's original placeholder art was KayKit + Kenney CC0 packs. All of it has since been replaced by the custom day/night asset pack built in `../asset-pack/` (see its `MASTER_ART_DIRECTION.md` and `homestead-defense/README.md`'s "Custom asset pack integration" section) — as of the House/Storehouse/Homestead kit-bash and the `sk_villager`/`sm_scaffold` additions, **no third-party art ships in the game anymore.**

The KayKit and Kenney source files remain in `docs/assets/` for historical reference (and because a few of this repo's dev-only tooling comments still mention them by name), but nothing in any `.tscn`/`.tres` scene loads them. Both packs were CC0 (public domain); their license files are still alongside them in `docs/assets/` as a thank-you, even though attribution isn't required and they're no longer in use:

- KayKit: Adventurers Character Pack (2.0) — `docs/assets/KayKit/KayKit_Adventurers_2.0_FREE/License.txt`
- KayKit: Medieval Hexagon Pack (1.0) — `docs/assets/KayKit/KayKit_Medieval_Hexagon_Pack_1.0_FREE/KayKit_Medieval_Hexagon_Pack_1.0_FREE/License.txt`
- KayKit: Dungeon Remastered (1.1) — `docs/assets/KayKit/KayKit_DungeonRemastered_1.1_FREE/KayKit_DungeonRemastered_1.1_FREE/License.txt`
- Kenney: Isometric Miniature Farm (2.0) — `docs/assets/kennys/kenney_isometric-miniature-farm/License.txt`
- Kenney: Animal Pack Remastered — `docs/assets/kennys/kenney_animal-pack-remastered/License.txt`

All in-game art is now the custom pack (`assets/custom_pack/`) — see `../asset-pack/README.md` for its own build/attribution notes (it's an original asset pack generated by this project's headless Blender pipeline, not third-party).
